02 / WHAT WE CHECKSTRUCTURED DATA, NOT MARKETING CLAIMS

Four checks that change the conversation.

01

Who could move tokens?

Extract spender or recipient from recognized Permit, Permit2 and ERC-20 approve requests. If Permit2 SignatureTransfer does not include a recipient, the tool flags that missing context.

02

How much and how long?

Flag uint256/uint160 maximum approvals, zero amounts, unreadable values, expired and unusually long deadlines. Display USDC units only for Circle's known Base contract.

03

Which chain and contract?

Show EIP-712 chainId and verifyingContract or the calldata target. Compare them against addresses you independently expect; warn about missing fields or mismatches.

04

What remains unknown?

Custom contracts, Permit2 transfer recipients supplied later, dynamic calldata, approvals previously granted and real-world trust cannot be resolved from one unsigned JSON blob.

⚠
Not a transaction simulator or revocation tool.

For live allowance inspection and revocation, use an independently verified service such as Revoke.cash. SignScope does not connect to your wallet or modify approvals. It does not guarantee protection from phishing.
